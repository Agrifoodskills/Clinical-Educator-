--- slide
id: ce01-03
layout: Split
title: Adult learners in clinical settings
left:
  type: ul
  color: accent1
  items:
    - text: "**They bring experience**"
      items:
        - Build on it, never ignore it
    - text: "**They need relevance**"
      items:
        - Anchor every point to a case
    - text: "**They self-assess poorly under stress**"
      items:
        - Feedback matters more, not less
right:
  type: ul
  color: accent2
  items:
    - text: "**The clinic pushes back**"
      items:
        - Interruptions are the norm
        - Patients come first, always
        - Teaching moments are unplanned
    - "*Design for 5-minute episodes, not 50-minute lectures*"
notes: |
  Contrast andragogy principles with clinical reality. Ask the group for
  their worst interrupted-teaching story before showing the right column.

--- slide
id: ce01-04
layout: Cards
title: One teaching episode, four moves
head1: "Plan"
card1:
  type: ul
  items:
    - Pick ONE teachable point
    - Know the learner's stage
head2: "Brief"
card2:
  type: ul
  items:
    - Set the task and the limits
    - "**Safety word** for handover"
head3: "Teach"
card3:
  type: ul
  items:
    - Learner does, teacher observes
    - Interrupt only for safety
head4: "Debrief"
card4:
  type: ul
  items:
    - Learner speaks first
    - One thing to keep, one to change
notes: |
  The whole session hangs off this cycle; the next two sessions deepen
  Brief and Debrief respectively.
